Account security basics

  • Use a unique, strong password (12+ characters, mix of letters, numbers, symbols) and do not reuse it elsewhere.
  • Enable two-factor authentication if StripChat offers it.
  • Never enter your StripChat password on a third-party site. Use only stripchat.com.
  • Log out from shared or public devices and avoid saving passwords in shared browsers.

Payment safety

  • Verify that payment pages use HTTPS and the correct domain.
  • Check your payment history regularly for unfamiliar charges.
  • Use payment methods that offer fraud protection where possible.
  • Be wary of external pages asking for direct transfers, gift cards, or crypto to personal wallets for “token deals.”

Privacy and data minimization

  • Review profile visibility, favorites, and followed creators settings.
  • Avoid sharing personal information in public chat: real name, address, workplace, phone number.
  • Consider using a dedicated email address for adult platforms.
  • Keep your operating system and browser updated to reduce malware risk.
